# D-090 — Product harmonic discovery is high-recall and non-authoritative

status: active
date: 2026-09-24

## Decision

HT-CN separates harmonic **discovery** from harmonic **Source identity**.

The Stable product may expose deterministic, high-recall XABC discovery candidates that satisfy source structural topology/envelopes and project PRZ through the existing Source machinery even when they do not yet satisfy every discrete canonical identity gate. Discovery may retain recent candidates across later pivot confirmations and may use bounded graph paths that skip one complete minor swing pair.

Discovery candidates are presentation/research objects only. They cannot:
- mutate or rescue CARNEY_RULES identity;
- rewrite Source Raw PRZ;
- fabricate a D point;
- create Source Terminal / Type-I / Type-II state;
- write M4 prospective evidence or Outcome state;
- make win-rate/alpha/profitability claims.

## Rationale

Using strict completed identity as the product's existence gate produced systematic false negatives and made Stable charts appear empty. Pine behavior demonstrated that useful operator workflow starts from persistent XABC + projected PRZ, then separates later completion/confirmation. This decision adopts that separation without weakening frozen Source truth.
